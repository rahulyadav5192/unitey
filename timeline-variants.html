Journey

A Journey of Growth

From founding vision to multi-entity sovereign infrastructure company built on conviction, purpose, and scale.

2021

Unitey Founded

Unitey was founded by payment industry veterans with a vision to build inclusive, interoperable, and sovereign financial ecosystems.

2022

Acquisition of Mercury

Together with Discover Financial Services and Hard Yaka, Unitey acquired Mercury a leading UAE payment network with more than 400,000 customers and global acceptance.

2023

Sovereign Infrastructure Role Expands

Mercury expanded its role by supporting governments in building and operating sovereign financial infrastructure.

2024

Retail Payment Services License & Shukria Launch

Mercury was awarded the Retail Payment Services License by the Central Bank of the UAE and launched Shukria to strengthen merchant services.

2025

MPS Global & PAPSSCARD

MPS Global was incorporated to scale its Scheme in a Box model across the Middle East and Africa. In partnership with Afreximbank, it launched PAPSSCARD, a pan-African card scheme.

2025

Unitey Business Services Established

Unitey Business Services was established as a joint venture with the Central Bank of the UAE to support the nation's Financial Infrastructure Transformation programme.

2026

Continued Expansion

Expands the ecosystem with new regional offices, deeper merchant services, and additional central bank partnerships across the Middle East and Africa.
