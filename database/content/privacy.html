1. Introduction

Unitey Digital Holdings Limited (“Unitey”, “we”, “our” or “us”) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting Personal Data in accordance with applicable data protection laws and regulations.

Unitey is incorporated in the Dubai International Financial Centre (“DIFC”), Dubai, United Arab Emirates. Unitey operates as a strategic holding company investing in, developing and supporting businesses focused on financial technology, payment infrastructure and related technology and services.

This Privacy Policy explains how Unitey collects, uses, processes, stores, shares and protects Personal Data when individuals:

  • visit or interact with Unitey’s website (the “Website”);
  • contact or communicate with Unitey;
  • submit enquiries through the Website;
  • engage with Unitey in a business or professional capacity; or
  • otherwise interact with Unitey.

Personal Data is processed in accordance with the DIFC Data Protection Law No. 5 of 2020, as amended from time to time, the DIFC Data Protection Regulations, and other applicable data protection and privacy requirements.

This Privacy Policy applies to Unitey and its Website. Unitey’s portfolio and operating companies are separate legal entities and may have their own privacy policies governing the collection and processing of Personal Data in connection with their respective products, services and operations.

2. Definitions

For the purposes of this Privacy Policy:

“Personal Data” means any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person.

“Controller” means the person or entity that, alone or jointly with others, determines the purposes and means of Processing Personal Data.

“Processing” means any operation or set of operations performed on Personal Data, including its collection, recording, organisation, storage, use, disclosure, transfer, alteration or deletion.

3. Personal Data We Collect

The Personal Data collected by Unitey will depend on the nature of an individual’s interaction with us.

We may collect the following categories of Personal Data:

3.1 Contact Information

This may include:

  • name;
  • email address;
  • telephone or mobile number;
  • business or correspondence address;
  • country or location; and
  • preferred method of communication.

3.2 Business and Professional Information

Where individuals interact with Unitey in a professional or business capacity, we may collect:

  • employer or organisation name;
  • job title or professional role;
  • business contact details;
  • information relating to an organisation or business;
  • details regarding the nature of an enquiry, proposal or potential business relationship; and
  • correspondence and communications with Unitey.

3.3 Website and Technical Information

When individuals access or use the Website, certain technical information may be collected automatically, including:

  • Internet Protocol (IP) address;
  • browser type and version;
  • device and operating system information;
  • device identifiers;
  • pages viewed and navigation patterns;
  • date and time of access;
  • referring website or source; and
  • system logs and diagnostic information.

3.4 Communications and Enquiry Information

Where individuals contact Unitey, we may process information contained in:

  • Website contact or enquiry forms;
  • emails and other correspondence;
  • business or partnership enquiries;
  • requests for information;
  • feedback; and
  • other communications submitted to Unitey.

Individuals are requested to provide only such Personal Data as is reasonably necessary for the purpose of their enquiry or interaction with Unitey.

4. How We Collect Personal Data

Unitey may collect Personal Data:

  • directly from individuals;
  • through the Website;
  • through email or other business communications;
  • through business meetings and professional interactions;
  • from organisations represented by an individual;
  • through service providers supporting the Website or Unitey’s operations; and
  • from publicly available or legitimate business sources where permitted by applicable law.

Unitey may process Personal Data for purposes including:

5.1 Responding to Enquiries

To receive, assess and respond to enquiries, requests for information and communications submitted through the Website or other channels.

5.2 Business and Strategic Relationships

To establish, administer and manage relationships with portfolio companies, prospective business partners, financial institutions, government bodies, vendors, professional advisers and other stakeholders.

5.3 Evaluating Business Opportunities

To assess potential partnerships, investments, strategic initiatives and other legitimate corporate or commercial opportunities.

5.4 Website Administration and Improvement

Unitey may process technical and usage information to support the effective operation, security and continuous improvement of the Website. This may include:

  • operating and maintaining the Website and its functionality;
  • monitoring Website performance, availability and usage;
  • understanding how visitors access and interact with the Website;
  • identifying and resolving technical issues;
  • improving Website functionality, content and overall user experience; and
  • maintaining the security and integrity of the Website and associated systems.

5.5 Corporate Governance and Administration

To support Unitey’s internal administration, governance, record keeping, risk management, audit and other legitimate corporate activities.

5.6 Legal and Regulatory Compliance

To comply with applicable legal, regulatory, judicial or governmental requirements and to establish, exercise or defend legal rights or claims.

5.7 Security and Fraud Prevention

To protect Unitey, its Website, systems and information from unauthorised access, cyber threats, misuse, fraud or other unlawful activity.

6. Lawful Basis for Processing

Unitey processes Personal Data only where there is an appropriate lawful basis under applicable data protection law.

Depending on the circumstances, this may include processing:

  • with the individual’s consent;
  • where necessary for the performance of, or steps taken in connection with, a contract;
  • where necessary to comply with a legal obligation;
  • where necessary to protect the vital interests of an individual;
  • where necessary for the performance of a task carried out in the interests of the DIFC or in the exercise of applicable functions or powers; or
  • where necessary for the legitimate interests pursued by Unitey or a relevant third party, provided that such interests do not improperly override the rights and interests of the individual.

Where processing is based on consent, individuals may withdraw their consent in accordance with applicable law.

Unitey may share Personal Data where reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy.

Recipients may include:

Personal Data may be shared with Unitey’s portfolio, affiliated or related companies where necessary for legitimate business purposes, including where an enquiry relates specifically to the activities or services of such an entity.

Service Providers

Unitey may engage third parties to provide services including Website hosting, information technology, cybersecurity, communications, data storage and other operational support.

Professional Advisers

Personal Data may be shared with lawyers, auditors, accountants, consultants and other professional advisers where reasonably required.

Authorities

Unitey may disclose Personal Data to courts, regulators, governmental authorities or law-enforcement bodies where required or permitted by applicable law.

Corporate Transactions

Personal Data may be disclosed where reasonably necessary in connection with an actual or proposed investment, financing, restructuring, merger, acquisition, disposal or other corporate transaction, subject to appropriate confidentiality and data-protection safeguards.

Unitey takes reasonable steps to ensure that parties processing Personal Data on its behalf are subject to appropriate confidentiality, security and data-protection requirements.

8. Cookies and Similar Technologies

The Website may use cookies and similar technologies to support Website functionality, security and performance and to understand how visitors interact with the Website.

Due to the international nature of Unitey’s activities, Personal Data may, where necessary, be transferred to or processed by recipients located outside the DIFC.

Where Personal Data is transferred outside the DIFC, Unitey will take appropriate measures to ensure that the transfer complies with applicable requirements of the DIFC Data Protection Law.

Depending on the circumstances, such measures may include transferring Personal Data to jurisdictions recognised as providing an adequate level of protection or implementing appropriate contractual, organisational or other safeguards permitted under applicable law.

10. Data Security

Unitey implements appropriate technical and organisational measures designed to protect Personal Data against accidental or unlawful destruction, loss, alteration, unauthorised disclosure, access or misuse.

Such measures may include, as appropriate:

  • secure communication and storage technologies;
  • network and system security controls;
  • appropriate confidentiality requirements;
  • security monitoring; and
  • periodic review of relevant security arrangements.

While Unitey takes reasonable measures to protect Personal Data, no electronic transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

11. Data Retention

Unitey retains Personal Data only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes for which it was collected and to satisfy applicable legal, regulatory, contractual, accounting, reporting or legitimate business requirements.

Retention periods may vary depending on the nature of the Personal Data and the purpose for which it is processed.

When Personal Data is no longer required, Unitey will take appropriate steps to securely delete or anonymise it, subject to applicable legal requirements.
