Our Journey

The Evolution of Unitey

From founding vision to multi-entity sovereign infrastructure company.

2021
Unitey Founded
Unitey was founded by payment industry veterans with a vision to build inclusive, interoperable, and sovereign financial ecosystems.
2022
Acquisition of Mercury
Unitey, together with Discover Financial Services (Capital One) and Hard Yaka, acquired Mercury, a leading UAE payment network with more than 400,000 payment cards and global acceptance.
2023
Sovereign Infrastructure Role Expands
Mercury expanded its role by supporting governments in building and operating sovereign financial infrastructure.
2024
Retail Payment Services License & Shukria
Mercury was awarded the Retail Payment Services License by the Central Bank of the UAE and launched Shukria to strengthen merchant services.
2025
MPS Global & PAPSSCard
MPS Global was incorporated to scale its Scheme in a Box across the Middle East and Africa. PAPSSCard was launched the same year, in partnership with Afreximbank, to establish a PanAfrican card scheme.
2025
Unitey Business Services
Unitey Business Services was established as a joint venture with the Central Bank of the UAE to support the nation's Financial Infrastructure Transformation programme.
2025
Mercury Secures Open Finance License
Mercury received deemed approval from the Central Bank of the UAE for its Open Finance license, connecting banks, fintechs, and enterprises through its Open Finance platform.
2026
Shukria Goes Live
Shukria commenced operations in the UAE, bringing merchant acquiring and card issuing services under the Shukria brand.

Founder's Perspective

A long-term view
of financial progress.

Unitey was founded on the belief that the next generation of financial infrastructure should strengthen local economies while expanding access to the opportunities of a digital financial system. We believe payment sovereignty will play an increasingly important role in how markets develop - creating greater control, resilience, and independence across critical payment infrastructure while maintaining global connectivity. This perspective continues to shape Unitey's strategy and long-term direction.
